Open the leaderboard

  1. Open Sky Diving X on your phone or tablet.
  2. From the main menu, choose Leaderboards. Your total feet skydived appears on that screen.
  3. Tap View on Game Center on iOS or View on Play Games on AndroidView on Game CenterView on Play Games.
  4. Use your device’s game-service profile when prompted. Platform rankings require that service to be available.

Your progress goes with your play

Your personal distance and game progress are stored on your device. The shared leaderboard opens inside the native app through its platform game service; it has no public browser scoreboard link.
